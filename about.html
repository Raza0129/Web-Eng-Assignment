Since 2014

From a Small Kitchen to a City Favourite

Azure Bistro & Grill opened its doors in 2014 with a single goal: to serve honest, beautifully prepared food in a space that feels like home. What started as a 20-seat corner restaurant has since grown into three locations across the city.

Our founders, trained in classical European kitchens, wanted to create a place where technique meets comfort — where a weeknight dinner can feel just as special as a celebration.
